Important Safety Information
Consult your healthcare provider before use if you:
Are pregnant or nursing
Have any active skin conditions, infections, or open wounds
Have a history of skin sensitivities or photosensitivity
Take medications that may cause photosensitivity (certain antibiotics, diuretics, or retinoids)
Have implanted medical devices such as pacemakers or defibrillators
Have any chronic health conditions
Have a history of skin cancer or precancerous lesions
Experience seizures or have photosensitive epilepsy
Are seeking treatment for a specific medical condition
Eye Protection: Do not look directly into the light source. Use protective eyewear if provided or recommended by the manufacturer. Close your eyes during facial treatments.
Skin Monitoring: While the treatment should be painless, discontinue use immediately if you experience any unusual skin reactions, discomfort, redness that doesn't resolve quickly, or irritation. Consult your healthcare provider if concerns arise.
General Safety: The device produces non-ionizing infrared light, which is different from ionizing radiation (such as UV or X-rays). However, proper usage according to manufacturer guidelines is essential for safe and effective results.

Understanding the Technology
Wavelength Spectrum Explained
Different wavelengths of light penetrate tissue to different depths:
590-660nm (visible red): Primarily affect superficial skin layers, supporting surface cell activity
660-760nm (red to near-infrared transition): Penetrate into upper dermal layers
760-900nm (near-infrared): Reach deeper dermal and subcutaneous tissues
900-1250nm (deeper near-infrared): Penetrate to the deepest levels accessible through phototherapy
By delivering this full spectrum simultaneously, the COLLAGENTEX addresses multiple tissue depths in a single session—supporting comprehensive skin wellness from surface to deeper structures.
Plasma-Arc Technology
The Plasma-Arc/Quartz lamp technology differs fundamentally from other light therapy approaches:
vs. Lasers: Lasers produce monochromatic (single wavelength), collimated (parallel beam) light. The COLLAGENTEX produces multiple wavelengths in a broad, non-collimated pattern for even coverage.
vs. LEDs: Light-emitting diodes typically offer quasi-monochromatic output with narrow spectral bands (±20nm around peak wavelength). The COLLAGENTEX provides a much broader spectrum spanning 660nm.
vs. IPL: Intense Pulsed Light systems deliver high-intensity, brief pulses of broad-spectrum light that create controlled thermal effects. The COLLAGENTEX operates continuously at lower intensity without thermal damage mechanisms.
The patented filter system shapes the lamp's output to emphasize therapeutically relevant wavelengths while managing heat and ensuring consistent performance across the bulb's lifespan.
Non-Collimated Light Advantage
The non-collimated (diverging) light pattern creates several practical benefits:
Easier positioning and coverage of treatment areas
More forgiving of distance variations during use
Even exposure across facial contours or body curves
Reduced risk of excessive intensity in any single spot
Natural, comfortable treatment experience
This design makes the device user-friendly for both professionals treating clients and individuals using it at home.
