The SolRx E-Series is a modular UVB-Narrowband phototherapy system designed for skin health support and vitamin D synthesis. Health Canada and FDA-compliant, this system can be expanded from a 2-bulb panel to a wraparound configuration for greater coverage. UVB-Narrowband therapy is commonly recommended by healthcare providers for individuals with conditions such as psoriasis or vitamin D deficiency when natural sunlight exposure is limited.
When paired with Crystal Quartz Panels, the light is naturally diffused, offering users a gentler experience. These panels are an optional enhancement designed to integrate seamlessly with SolRx E-Series devices.
