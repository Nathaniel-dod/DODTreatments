His understanding of the body began long before his career in natural medicine. As a professional gymnast, Dr. Sage experienced firsthand both the extraordinary capabilities of the human body and the consequences of asking it to perform beyond its limits. That experience sparked a deeper interest in how the body adapts, compensates, breaks down and recovers.

Today, Dr. Sage teaches internationally, working with an extraordinarily diverse range of people—from teenagers, parents and retirees to professional trainers, massage therapists, nurses, chiropractors, naturopaths, medical doctors and oncologists. This unique role places him at the intersection of practitioner education, hands-on experience, and the broader philosophy behind the Wolfe Non Surgical approach that eliminates the need for most surgeries. This is the NATURAL course of real health, real healing and total recovery.
But Dr. Sage's perspective extends beyond physical bodywork.

He has created a unique philosophy when it comes to the relationship between the physical body and the mind, and in how beliefs, psychology, lifestyle, movement and personal responsibility can influence the way people experience their health has made him a leader in the field of body mechanics.

His philosophy challenges people to become more personally involved in their own bodies' healing rather than automatically assuming that pain, physical limitation or declining function are simply inevitable consequences of aging.

Whether discussing chronic pain, human performance, recovery, mobility, aging, psychology, natural health or the body's capacity for adaptation, Dr. Sage brings a perspective shaped by years of working directly with the human body and teaching others how to understand it.

His central message is simple but provocative:
Before assuming that the body is broken, we should understand what it is trying to tell us.
Dr. Sage is the ideal guest for conversations exploring what the human body is capable of, why modern society has become increasingly disconnected from their bodies, and what it means to take massive personal action in understanding and caring for our own health. Your body is a gift.
