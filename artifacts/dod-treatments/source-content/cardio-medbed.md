Clinically Validated – Studies confirm EECP's effectiveness in patients with coronary artery disease.

## How The Cardio MedBed Works

Cardio MedBed Treatment is administered through three pairs of external inflatable cuffs that are applied around the lower legs, upper legs and buttocks. These cuffs continuously inflate and deflate between the resting period of the heartbeat and increase blood returned to the heart.

##### Step 1

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a036c393e53022ce2ad6e26/6a037139b314a7a133789249_cardiomed_how_works_01%201.avif)

Inflation initiates retro- grade pulse wave

##### Step 2

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a036c393e53022ce2ad6e26/6a037139b314a7a13378924d_cardiomed_how_works_01%202.avif)

Inflation of lower thigh cuffs 50ms later

##### Step 3

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a036c393e53022ce2ad6e26/6a037139b314a7a133789241_cardiomed_how_works_01%203.avif)

Inflation of upper thigh cuffs 50ms later

##### Step 4

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a036c393e53022ce2ad6e26/6a037139b314a7a133789245_cardiomed_how_works_01%204.avif)

Deflation facilitates cardiac unloading

##### How does Cardio MedBed increase blood flow?

It stimulates the openings or formation of small branches of blood vessels to create a natural bypass around narrowed or blocked arteries.
