---
name: Visible contact details
description: Presentation rule for contact and booking actions.
---

Whenever a page asks visitors to call or email, display the actual phone number or email address in visible text rather than hiding it behind a generic action label.

**Why:** Visitors need to see and verify contact details before choosing how to reach out.

**How to apply:** Use visible, readable contact values inside booking cards, contact panels, and linked call/email buttons while preserving clickable tel and mailto behavior.
