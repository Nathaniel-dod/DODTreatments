---
name: Treatment claim voice
description: Approved voice and audience assumptions for treatment benefit copy.
---

Strong treatment-benefit statements should be framed as attributed experience, using language such as “Patients have noticed…”, “We have had patients say…”, or “Some patients report…”, rather than guaranteed or absolute outcomes.

**Why:** The brand wants persuasive experiential language while softening its harshest claims. Most site traffic is warm and already understands the treatments, so generic introductory or “who it may suit” explanations add unnecessary friction.

**How to apply:** Use this convention when writing or revising treatment, clinic, and SEO copy. Preserve the confident premium tone, qualify outcomes through patient attribution, and do not add basic treatment primers unless specifically requested.
